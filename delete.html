My Ihsan: Delete your data

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · App: My Ihsan by Ihsan Rahim (package com.myihsan.myapp) · Contact: ihsanrahim@gmail.com

My Ihsan has no accounts and no server of ours. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, so you can delete all of it yourself, at once, without asking us.

Delete everything on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings › Apps › My Ihsan › Storage.
  2. Tap Clear data (or Clear storage) and confirm. Uninstalling the app does the same.

This deletes, immediately and permanently: settings, prayer and rakaat records, notes, family tree, baby and Care records (medicines, vital signs, appointments), finance entries, School progress, exercise, period and fasting records, recipes, books and photos saved in the app. If you made a backup file yourself (Settings › Backup), delete that file too.

Groups and shared Split bill

  • To remove a post: open it in the group and tap Delete.
  • To leave: open the group and tap Leave group. Its posts are removed from your phone.
  • Posts are encrypted on your phone before they leave it. Public Nostr relay servers (not run by us) hold only the encrypted copies; without the group's invite link nobody, including us, can read them. Relays drop old data on their own schedule.

Hilal reports (Astronomy)

A sighting report is public and anonymous (no name, location rounded to about 50 km), so it cannot be traced back to you or deleted by person.

What we keep

Nothing. We do not collect personal data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on a server. Questions: ihsanrahim@gmail.com.
