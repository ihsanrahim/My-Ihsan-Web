My Ihsan: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · App: My Ihsan (package com.myihsan.myapp) · Contact: ihsanrahim@gmail.com

My Ihsan is an Islamic companion app (prayer times, Quran, doa, Hajj and Umrah guide, halal checker, family tree, finance notes, exercise and more). We built it to keep your information on your own phone. We have no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not run a server that collects your data.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you enter or the app records is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage: your name and settings, prayer and rakaat counts, notes, family tree and birthdays, baby and event records, finance entries (debts, stocks, gold, wasiat), exercise logs and body measurements, saved masjid locations, and your app lock PIN (stored as a hash). We cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted. Android backup may copy it to your Google account if you have Android backup turned on.

2. Permissions and how they are used

Each permission is optional and asked for only when you turn on the feature.

  • Location (precise, approximate): to find your JAKIM prayer zone, qibla direction and nearby masjid. Your position is used on the phone. See section 3 for the two cases where it is sent to a service.
  • Travel: run and ride (Exercise › Travel, optional): precise location is read once a second only while a run is going and the app is open on screen; it stops when you finish or leave the app. The route, times and the ids of photos you take stay on the phone. A picture or video of a run leaves the phone only when you tap Share. The optional satellite map loads map pictures from Esri World Imagery (server.arcgisonline.com), which sees your IP address and the map area being shown; switch "Satellite map" off to avoid this.
  • Background location: only if you turn on "auto silent at masjid". The phone uses geofences to notice you arriving at or leaving a masjid or surau you saved. The location is not stored or uploaded.
  • Do Not Disturb access: only to silence the phone during prayer or at a saved masjid, and restore it afterwards.
  • Camera: for the qibla camera view, counting rakaat by detecting sujud, and scanning barcodes. Frames are processed on the phone and are not saved or uploaded. (If you choose the halal photo reading feature, see section 3.)
  • Microphone: only for the optional "read the syahadah with the microphone" step and the optional 🎙️ listen button in School (Sekolah) oral reading, where the words heard are compared on the phone to suggest a mark. It uses your phone's speech recognition service, which may be provided by Google or your phone maker and has its own privacy policy.
  • Notifications and exact alarms, boot completed, foreground services, wake lock, vibrate: to deliver prayer-time alerts and reminders on time (including after the phone restarts), and for the rakaat counter, radio and audio player.
  • Physical activity: to count your steps, on the phone.
  • Usage access: only if you turn on screen-time statistics. App usage is read on the phone and not sent anywhere.
  • Internet: for the online features in section 3.

3. When information leaves your phone

Only when you use these features, and only what the feature needs:

  • Prayer times: your zone code is sent to JAKIM e-Solat (e-solat.gov.my) to get the timetable.
  • Umrah itinerary maker: place searches and map data go to OpenStreetMap-based services (Photon by Komoot, Overpass API, OpenFreeMap tiles). The text you search and the map position are sent to them.
  • Halal checker, barcode: the scanned barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts.
  • Ask AI and halal photo reading (optional): your question, or the photo or text of the ingredient label you choose, is sent to the AI service you select. By default this is Pollinations (no account). You may choose OpenRouter, Google Gemini, DeepSeek or another service and enter your own key, which is stored only on your phone. These services have their own privacy policies. Do not send anything you want to keep private.
  • Groups (Kumpulan, optional): posts, polls, events and replies you write in a group are encrypted on your phone (AES-GCM) with the group's key, which only members holding the invite link have. The encrypted messages are sent to public Nostr relay servers (relay.damus.io, nos.lol, relay.primal.net) so other members can fetch them. The relays cannot read the content, but they see your IP address, a random key made for this app on your phone (not your name or phone number), the group's random tag and the time of each message. A background job fetches new messages for your groups and shows notifications. Leaving a group removes it from your phone; messages already on the relays stay there in encrypted form. "Translate" sends the post's text to the AI service you chose (see below).
  • School (Sekolah): questions, answers, progress, exam history, child names and settings stay on the phone. Nothing about your children is sent anywhere.
  • Display (Paparan) weather, optional: the display's coordinates (your location or a masjid you set) are sent to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) to get the forecast.
  • Radio, podcasts and Quran audio: your device connects directly to the stream hosts, which can see your IP address.
  • Links: Google Maps, WhatsApp, airlines and other sites open in their own app or browser, under their own policies.

We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Children

The app is a general audience family app. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Family and baby records, and School (Sekolah) progress for each child, are entered by the parent and stay on the phone. Children's names and answers are never sent to any server.

5. Your choices

You can deny or withdraw any permission in Android settings. You can delete everything with Settings > Apps > My Ihsan > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Religious content

Arabic text, translations, doa and hadith references are provided in good faith and are being reviewed by a qualified teacher. Please check with a scholar for rulings. The halal checker is a guide only and not a fatwa.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears above and the update notes in the app will say so.
