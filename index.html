My Ihsan: Privacy Policy

App: My Ihsan (Android, package com.myihsan.myapp) · Effective date: 9 November 2026 · Applies to version 0.4.0 and later
In short: My Ihsan keeps your information on your own phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and we do not run a server that collects your data. Information leaves your phone only when you use an online feature, and then it goes directly from your phone to the service that feature needs (listed below). We never sell your data.

My Ihsan is an Islamic companion app for daily ibadah and Muslim life: prayer times, qibla, Al-Quran, doa and zikir, Hajj and Umrah guides, a halal checker and halal recipe builder, a period (haid) tracker, Books & PDF tools, family, finance, fitness, calendar sync, radio and more. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, where it goes and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean the developer of My Ihsan.

1. Information stored only on your phone

Everything you enter, and everything the app records, is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see or access it. This includes:

  • Your name, language, settings and prayer zone;
  • Prayer, rakaat, zikir and Quran progress, journal entries and notes;
  • Period (haid) tracker data: cycle dates, nifas, istihadhah, predictions, fasts to replace and prayers to make up, and related health notes;
  • Fitness and health data: step counts, exercise logs, routines and body measurements;
  • Finance data: expenses, debts, savings, zakat, gold, stock notes and wasiat;
  • Family tree, birthdays, children's profiles, baby records and photos, events and greeting cards;
  • Recipes, meal plans and shopping lists you create;
  • PDFs, books and photos you add to Books, and the edits you make to them;
  • Saved masjid and surau locations;
  • Your app lock and kids-lock PIN (stored as a hash, not as plain text);
  • Any AI service keys you choose to enter (kept on the phone and sent only to that service).

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all of this is deleted. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account and its privacy policy.

Each permission is requested only when you turn on the feature that needs it. You can refuse or withdraw any of them in Android Settings and the rest of the app keeps working.

3. When information leaves your phone

Only when you use these features, and only what the feature needs. Requests go directly from your phone to the service; they do not pass through any server of ours. Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address.

Prayer times, Quran and religious content

  • Prayer times: your prayer zone code is sent to JAKIM e-Solat (e-solat.gov.my) to download the timetable.
  • Al-Quran text, translations, tafsir and audio: downloaded from Al Quran Cloud, Quran.com, EveryAyah and Internet Archive. Only the requested surah or verse is sent.

Maps, Umrah planner and travel

  • Place searches and map positions are sent to OpenStreetMap-based services: Photon by Komoot (search), Overpass API (places) and OpenFreeMap (map tiles).
  • Currency rates are downloaded from Frankfurter (no personal data is sent).

Halal checker

  • Barcode lookup: the scanned barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts.
  • Ingredient photo or text checks with AI (optional): see "AI features" below.

Halal recipe builder

  • When you share a link to My Ihsan (for example from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest or a recipe website), the app downloads that page, and where available its video, captions or audio, from that website to read the recipe. That website receives a normal request from your phone.
  • If you use the in-app reader and sign in to a website, the sign-in cookies stay in the app on your phone and are only sent to that website.
  • Recipe text, photos, video still frames and audio are sent to an AI or speech-to-text service only if you turn AI on (see below).

AI features (optional)

Ask AI, halal photo reading, recipe building from photos or videos, and text recognition (OCR) of book pages are optional. When you use them, the question, text, photo, page image, video frames or audio you choose is sent to the AI service you select. By default this is Pollinations (no account needed). You may instead choose OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud (Qwen) or another service and enter your own key. You can also choose to open your own installed AI app (such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) instead, in which case the text is handed to that app. These services process your content under their own terms and privacy policies, which we do not control. Please do not send anything you want to keep private, and do not send period, health or financial details to an AI service unless you are comfortable with that service's policy.

Calendar sync (optional)

Events are written to the calendar you pick on your phone. If that calendar belongs to an account such as Google, Microsoft Outlook, Samsung or Exchange, that provider will sync the events to its servers under your account and its privacy policy. Period events use private titles by default (for example an emoji instead of words), and you can leave period events out entirely.

Radio, podcasts and media

  • Station and podcast lists are fetched from Radio Browser, gpodder.net and the Apple Podcasts directory; your search words are sent to them.
  • When you play a stream or episode, your phone connects directly to that host. A few radio streams use plain HTTP; these carry no personal data.

Links to other apps and websites

Links to Google Maps, Waze, WhatsApp, YouTube, airlines, hotels, government sites and others open in their own app or browser and are covered by their own privacy policies.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not have user accounts and do not ask for your email or phone number.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs or files outside the ones you open in the app.

5. Who data is shared with

We do not receive your data, so we do not share it. The only parties that receive any data are the third-party services listed in section 3, and only when you use the matching feature. The types of parties are: government prayer-time services, Quran content providers, map and place-search providers, a food product database, AI and speech-to-text providers you select, your calendar provider, radio and podcast hosts, and websites whose links you share to the app.

6. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to online services use HTTPS (encrypted), except for some public radio streams. You can protect the app with the app lock PIN. Please also keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual items inside the app, remove everything with Android Settings › Apps › My Ihsan › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data already sent to a third-party service (for example an AI provider or your calendar account) is kept under that service's own policy; contact that service to have it deleted.

8. Children

My Ihsan is a general-audience family app and is not directed at children. It includes children's profiles and a kids lock that a parent sets up; that information is entered by the parent and stays on the phone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through an AI feature, contact the AI provider concerned, or contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because your data is held on your own device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app or in Android Settings. For data held by a third-party service, please use that service's own tools. You can contact us with any question.

10. Religious content

Arabic text, translations, doa, hadith references, fiqh guidance (including the period tracker) and the halal checker are provided in good faith as a guide only and are not a fatwa. Please consult a qualified scholar for rulings.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above, and the app's update notes will mention it. Significant changes will apply only to new versions of the app.
